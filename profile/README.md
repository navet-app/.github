## Your home, at a glance

Navet brings lights, climate, media, energy, security, and routines into an interface built for everyday use. Organize controls by room, shape your dashboards around your household, and choose the layout and appearance that suit each screen.

Navet is open source and self-hosted. It connects to **Home Assistant, Homey, and openHAB**, with features depending on each provider's supported capabilities. See the [provider compatibility guide](https://docs.navet.app/integrations/) for details.
